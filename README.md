## FAQ

### 1. Which iPhones are covered?

The project covers iPhone 7, 8, X, XR, XS, and 11, including their Plus, Pro, and Max variants.

### 2. What is the latest iOS for iPhone 7?

The iPhone 7 is limited to the iOS 15 generation. The latest listed release for this device family is iOS 15.8.8.

### 3. Can iPhone 8 install iOS 17?

No. iPhone 8 and iPhone 8 Plus are limited to the iOS 16 generation.

### 4. Can iPhone X install iOS 17?

No. iPhone X is limited to the iOS 16 generation.

### 5. Can iPhone XR and XS install iOS 27?

No. These models are limited to the iOS 18 generation.

### 6. Can iPhone 11 install iOS 27?

Yes. The iPhone 11 family is supported by iOS 27.

### 7. Which devices support checkm8-based jailbreak research?

Compatible A10 and A11 devices include iPhone 7, iPhone 8, and iPhone X.

### 8. Does palera1n work on every iPhone in this repository?

No. palera1n is intended for compatible checkm8 devices. It does not support iPhone XR, XS, or 11 through checkm8.

### 9. Can I downgrade to an older iOS version?

Standard restores generally require Apple to sign the target firmware. Some legacy workflows involving saved SHSH blobs have additional requirements and limitations.

### 10. What is the difference between IPSW and jailbreak?

IPSW is an Apple firmware package. Jailbreak is a separate method of modifying iOS security restrictions.

### 11. Can I install older applications on these iPhones?

Application availability depends on the app developer, App Store compatibility, and the minimum required iOS version.

### 12. Where should I download firmware?

Use trusted firmware catalogs that provide model-specific packages and verify Apple's signing status before restoring.
